Eric Silberger

Violinist, Conductor, Recording Producer, Video Producer

Biography

A performer's insight and a producer's precision — bringing technical mastery and musical depth to every recording.

A Performer's Insight and a Producer's Precision.

Eric Silberger is an internationally acclaimed violinist and an experienced audio and video producer, bringing a rare dual perspective to the production process. A prizewinner at the XIV International Tchaikovsky Competition and the Michael Hill International Violin Competition, he has appeared as a soloist with major orchestras including the London Philharmonia, Mariinsky Orchestra, and San Francisco Symphony. He is also an alumnus of the Perlman Music Program, an experience that helped shape his collaborative approach and deep commitment to musical excellence.

Alongside his performing career, Silberger has built extensive expertise in recording production, multi-camera video capture, and post-production workflows. He has led and collaborated on projects ranging from intimate chamber recordings to full orchestral productions, often designing end-to-end systems that integrate high-level audio engineering with cinematic visual presentation. His work prioritizes clarity, realism, and emotional impact — ensuring that the artistic intent translates fully to the final product.

Silberger has worked closely with leading artists, engineers, and institutions, and previously served as Head of Violin at tonebase, where he produced and conducted interviews with many of the world's top violinists. As a co-founder of the Hawaii International Music Festival, he has also overseen large-scale artistic and production operations, further strengthening his ability to bridge artistic vision with practical execution.

In collaboration with engineer James Connell, Silberger brings a performer's insight and a producer's precision to each project, helping shape recordings that are both technically exceptional and musically compelling.
